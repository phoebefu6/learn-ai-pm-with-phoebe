Learn AI Product Management with Phoebe

Decide what to build, and why

AI is excellent at the parts of product management that look like writing and useless at the part that is actually the job. It will draft a PRD from one sentence, and that PRD will read beautifully and decide nothing. This course is about the difference. A leader track on what AI changes in product work, what evidence has to mean now, and who signs - with no code to write. And a PM track that takes one real decision from a vague stakeholder ask to a signable spec with a metric, a boundary and a stated trade-off. The signature lab scores a spec against the six decisions that predict whether a build goes well - including the spec you paste into it.

The leader track 🤝 · for heads of product, founders & stakeholders · 6 x 45 min · no code to write

Thinking mode: what AI actually changes in product work and what it cannot touch, how to tell research from confabulation in your own team's documents, what a spec must contain before anyone signs it, prioritisation without theatre, metrics and the readout that admits it did not work, and how to fund an AI-assisted product org without hollowing out the craft. Each session ends with the questions to ask your PMs.

The PM track 🛠️ · for practitioners · 10 sessions x 45 min (b10: 60) · bring a real backlog item

One decision, start to finish. You are the PM on Cadence, the AI note-taker from this series - already branded, marketed and financed in the sibling courses, with nobody having decided what it builds next. This course owns discovery to decision and stops there on purpose. Charters, milestones, critical path, risk registers and status reports are learn-ai-project-management-with-phoebe, and b10 hands over to it explicitly. The two are a pair: product management decides what to build and why, project management gets it built. For the metrics side deeper than b7 and b8 go, see learn-metric-decomposition and learn-experimentation.

Built from PRD and discovery practice, jobs-to-be-done, opportunity-solution-tree thinking, and the research-quality literature - full source map and honest out-of-scope list in materials/official-course-map.md. The spec scorer's ladder (15 / 35 / 55 / 70 / 100) is measured rather than asserted: six real checks run over the actual text of five drafts.
